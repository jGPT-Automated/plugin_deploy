Why it matters.
A stronger game starts with people like you.
Participatory expansion
Players build the network by physically bringing missing courts online instead of LocalCheck simply populating a database.
Permanent history
Courts keep a visible record of who helped establish their LocalCheck identity, even as the community changes around them.
Naturally scarce
A court can have thousands of players and many Local Legends, but only one original Pioneer.
